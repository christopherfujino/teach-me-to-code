There is a paradigm shift between a hobbyist programmer and a professional
software engineer. Russ Cox says that "software engineering is what happens to
programming when you add time and other programmers."[^rsc] Because I did not
study computer science and didn't do any industry-related internships, making
the leap from someone who could make beeps and boops on my laptop to an
engineer that a company would entrust with prod access to all their customers'
data.

There are obvious differences, like writing design docs, unit tests,
documentation, and postmortems.

[^rsc]: [What is Software Engineering?](https://research.swtch.com/vgo-eng)
